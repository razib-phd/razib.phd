Research Profile

What I work on

I am an aquaculture and environmental scientist with over a decade of interdisciplinary research experience, focused on the intersection of antimicrobial resistance (AMR), aquatic environmental health, and sustainable food systems. My work investigates how aquaculture systems shape, and are shaped by, microbial ecology, environmental contaminants, and management practices. I earned my PhD through a double‑degree program at the University of Copenhagen (Denmark) and Patuakhali Science and Technology University (Bangladesh).

My research integrates molecular microbiology (qPCR, high‑throughput ARG/MGE profiling), analytical chemistry (GC‑MS, HPLC), environmental monitoring, and quantitative ecological analysis to address health and sustainability challenges in aquatic food production. I have worked within EU‑, USAID‑, and DANIDA‑funded projects, trained field teams, mentored students, and published 18 peer‑reviewed papers. My recent work centers on aquaculture resistomes, environmental AMR surveillance, and translating molecular evidence into actionable monitoring and management approaches.
